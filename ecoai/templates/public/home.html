Structure-aware rewriting

Code blocks, URLs and template placeholders are extracted before any rewriting happens and restored untouched. Line structure survives, so few-shot examples and lists still look like themselves.

A score that's measured

Every optimization reports a retention score: the share of content words, numbers and protected spans that survived. It is computed from the text, not asserted as a constant.

Transparent carbon math

Tokens to FLOPs to joules to grams CO₂e, with every coefficient documented and overridable through configuration. Regional grid intensity included.

How it works

1. Optimize

Send a prompt through the Studio or the SDK. Choose conservative, balanced or aggressive depending on how much rewriting you want.

2. Record

Each optimization produces a receipt: tokens before and after, energy, emissions, and exactly which transformations fired. 

from ecoai_sdk import EcoAI

eco = EcoAI(api_key="ecoai_...")
result = eco.optimize(
    "Could you please kindly summarize this?",
    strategy="balanced",
)

print(result.optimized)
print(result.tokens_saved, "tokens saved")
print(result.co2_g_saved, "g CO2e avoided")
About the numbers Emissions figures are estimates built from published coefficients, not measurements taken inside a provider's datacenter. They are reliable for comparing a prompt against itself before and after optimization, which is what the dashboard reports. The documentation shows every coefficient and where it comes from.
